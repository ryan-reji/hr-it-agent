
# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility

Employees who have completed at least 3 months of employment may request remote work arrangements.

Remote work eligibility depends on job responsibilities, departmental requirements, and manager approval.

Employees working in roles requiring physical office presence may not be eligible for regular remote work.

## 2. Work From Home Allowance

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week.

Additional remote work days require approval from the department head.

Employees must not assume that remote work is automatically approved.

## 3. Working Hours and Availability

Employees must follow their assigned working hours while working remotely.

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, with a one-hour lunch break.

Employees must remain reachable through approved communication platforms during working hours.

Employees must attend scheduled meetings and communicate availability to their teams.

## 4. Remote Work Security

Employees must connect to company systems using the approved corporate VPN.

Public Wi-Fi networks should not be used to access confidential company resources unless an approved secure connection is active.

Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

## 5. Equipment and Workspace

Employees must use company-approved devices for accessing internal systems.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and professional remote working environment.

Any loss, theft, or damage involving company equipment must be reported to IT immediately.

## 6. Remote Work Requests

Employees must submit remote work requests through the HR portal.

The reporting manager is responsible for reviewing and approving requests.

Approved arrangements may be modified based on operational requirements.
