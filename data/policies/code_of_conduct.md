
# Employee Code of Conduct

## 1. Professional Behaviour

Employees must maintain professional, respectful, and ethical behaviour in all workplace interactions.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, and abusive behaviour are prohibited.

Employees must treat colleagues, clients, vendors, and business partners with dignity and respect.

## 2. Workplace Communication

Employees must use professional language in company emails, meetings, and collaboration platforms.

Confidential business discussions must take place through approved communication channels.

Employees must not distribute offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate material using company resources.

## 3. Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or professional interest that may conflict with their responsibilities.

Employees must not use their company position to obtain unauthorized personal benefits.

Potential conflicts must be reported to HR for review.

## 4. Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information during and after their employment.

Business information must only be shared with individuals who have appropriate authorization.

Employees must not disclose client information, financial records, or internal business strategies without approval.

## 5. Reporting Misconduct

Employees who witness unethical behaviour, harassment, discrimination, or policy violations should report the matter to HR.

Reports may be submitted through the official HR reporting channel.

HR is responsible for reviewing reports and maintaining appropriate confidentiality.

## 6. Disciplinary Action

Violations of the code of conduct may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

Depending on the severity of the violation, actions may include formal warnings, access restrictions, suspension, or termination.

All disciplinary matters are reviewed by authorized HR personnel in accordance with company procedures.
