
# Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to 24 working days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave is credited at the beginning of each calendar year. Employees joining during the year receive leave on a prorated basis based on their joining date.

Annual leave can be used for vacations, personal commitments, and planned absences.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the following calendar year. Any balance exceeding this limit expires at the end of the year.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 working days of sick leave per calendar year.

Sick leave can be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or medical reasons.

For absences exceeding two consecutive working days, employees must submit a medical certificate to HR.

Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or converted into cash.

## 3. Leave Application Procedure

Employees must submit leave requests through the company's HR portal.

Planned annual leave requests should be submitted at least 7 calendar days before the intended leave date.

Emergency leave requests should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.

All leave requests require approval from the employee's reporting manager.

Employees must not consider leave approved until they receive confirmation through the HR portal or an authorized manager.

## 4. Leave Approval Rules

Managers review leave requests based on team availability, project deadlines, and operational requirements.

Requests exceeding 5 consecutive working days may require additional approval from the department head.

HR maintains official leave records and resolves discrepancies between employee balances and approved leave records.

## 5. Public Holidays and Weekends

Official public holidays and weekly non-working days are not deducted from annual leave balances.

Leave is calculated using working days only.

## 6. Leave Balance Queries

Employees can view their available annual and sick leave through the HR portal.

For incorrect leave balances, employees must contact HR with the relevant leave dates and approval records.

The HR department is responsible for investigating and correcting verified discrepancies.
