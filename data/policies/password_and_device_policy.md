
# Password and Device Policy

## 1. Password Requirements

Corporate passwords must contain at least 12 characters.

Passwords must include a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Passwords must not contain easily guessed information such as employee names, birthdays, or common dictionary words.

Employees must not reuse their corporate passwords across personal and professional services.

## 2. Password Management

Employees must never share passwords with colleagues, managers, or external parties.

Passwords must be stored only in company-approved password management tools.

If an employee suspects that their password has been compromised, they must change it immediately and notify IT.

## 3. Password Reset Procedure

Employees must use the official password reset portal to reset forgotten passwords.

If self-service password reset is unavailable, employees must raise an IT helpdesk ticket.

IT support personnel may verify employee identity before assisting with password recovery.

## 4. Company Device Usage

Company-issued laptops, mobile devices, and other equipment must be used primarily for authorized business activities.

Employees must protect devices from unauthorized physical and digital access.

Devices must be locked whenever left unattended.

## 5. Device Encryption and Updates

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.

Employees must not disable endpoint protection, antivirus, or security monitoring tools.

Operating system and security updates must be installed within the timelines communicated by IT.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Devices

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported immediately to the IT department.

IT may remotely lock or wipe devices to protect company information.

Employees must cooperate with IT during security investigations.

## 7. Device Return

Employees must return company equipment when requested by IT or HR, including during employment termination.

Company data must not be copied to personal devices before returning equipment.
